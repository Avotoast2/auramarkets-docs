auramarkets pages are yours to arrange. Two levels of control, both saved **per-account** so your setup follows you across devices:

## The Layout button

[Equities](https://auramarkets.io/indices), [Macro](https://auramarkets.io/macro), and [Today](https://auramarkets.io/today) each carry a **Layout** button in the page header. It opens one editor listing every section on the page:

- **Drag ☰** to reorder sections (within the reorderable area of each page).
- **✕ removes** a section from the page entirely — it drops into a *Removed* pool at the bottom of the editor.
- **＋ restores** a removed section; it reappears at the end of the list, ready to drag wherever you want it.
- **Reset to defaults** puts the whole page back to the shipped arrangement.

Changes apply instantly — no reload — and persist to your account.

What's arrangeable where:

| Page | Reorder + remove | Remove only |
|---|---|---|
| **Equities** | the entire panel area — Breadth, Volatility, Movers, Earnings, Highs & Lows, MA lists, Sectors & Industries, Rotation, Thematic Monitor | World Indices, Performance Chart, Market Intelligence sidebar |
| **Macro** | all five sections — Cross Asset Monitor, Credit, Upcoming Events, CTA Pulse, Deep Dive | Market Pulse sidebar |
| **Today** | the right column — Smart money, Rotation watch, On deck, Market color | Your names, Writer signal |

<Note>
Breadth and Volatility on Equities are **half-width blocks** — place them next to each other and they share a row; separate them and each pairs with whatever half-width neighbor follows. And if you remove Writer signal on Today, the right column takes over the full width rather than leaving a gap.
</Note>

## The gears

Tables with a **⚙** in their header let you pick the *contents*: which volatility metrics, which breadth dimensions, which world indices per region, which instruments per Cross Asset category. Every gear opens the same editor:

- Your **active items** at the top — drag ☰ to reorder, ✕ to remove.
- The **Available** pool below — ＋ to add.
- **Reset to defaults** restores the shipped set immediately.

Gears live on: the Equities **Volatility** panel, **Market Breadth** (including the five cockpit dimensions), each **World Indices** region, and every **Cross Asset Monitor** category on Macro.

## One editor everywhere

Layout modal and gears share a single interaction, so learning one means knowing them all. Section arrangement, table contents, chart preferences ([indicators and scales](/indices/stock-chart)), and control positions all persist to your account independently — reset one without touching the others.
