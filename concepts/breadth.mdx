**Breadth** answers one question: *are most stocks moving with the index, or is a small group of names doing all the work?*

A market that goes up on five mega-caps is more fragile than a market that goes up on four hundred names. Breadth tells you which kind of day it was.

## The metrics you'll see

### Advances / Declines (A/D)
How many stocks went up vs down. **Net A/D** = advancers minus decliners.
- Strongly positive (e.g., +300) = broad rally, real participation
- Strongly negative = broad sell-off
- Near zero on a big index move = only a few names moved the index

### % above moving average
What fraction of stocks are above their 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y MA. Stacked across timeframes:
- **% above 1W MA** = short-term momentum
- **% above 1M MA** = trend health
- **% above 1Y MA** = secular regime

The shorter the MA, the more sensitive — when % above 1W drops sharply but % above 1Y stays high, you're in a tactical pullback inside a structural uptrend.

### RSP / SPY ratio
Equal-weight S&P (RSP) divided by cap-weighted (SPY).
- **Falling RSP/SPY** = mega-caps carrying the index, narrow leadership
- **Rising RSP/SPY** = broad participation, healthy rally

## "Deteriorating breadth"

When you read this in a recap, it usually means one of:
- A/D net is shrinking even as the index goes up
- % above 1M or 3M MA is falling toward 50%
- RSP/SPY is dropping (concentration is increasing)

Why it matters: deteriorating breadth often **precedes corrections**. The index can stay green for weeks while internals rot, and then catch up suddenly.

## The breadth cockpit

The Equities page's **Market Breadth** panel condenses all of this into five dimensions, each shown with its current value, percentile vs its own history, and a sparkline:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Participation** | % of S&P 500 stocks above their 50-day MA |
| **Direction** | McClellan oscillator — momentum of daily advance/decline breadth |
| **Extremes** | Net new 52-week highs minus lows, as % of the universe |
| **Leadership** | Whether the names leading are broadening or narrowing |
| **Conviction** | Net count of 9:1 advance vs decline days over the last 10 sessions |

The panel synthesizes the five into **one state** — **HEALTHY** (broad participation confirms the trend), **THRUST** (a momentum ignition off a low), **WASHOUT** (capitulation readings, the contrarian zone), **DIVERGENCE** (index up while internals rot — the classic pre-correction pattern), or **NARROW** (a few names doing all the work) — with a one-line read naming what's driving it. Click any dimension to open it as a full chart.

### The Breadth Explorer

The cockpit's **Decompose →** link answers the follow-up question: *whose* breadth? The Explorer splits the same % - above - 50-day measure by **sector, size bucket, theme, or factor basket**, showing each group's contribution to the recent move and a two-year quilt of every group's breadth vs its own history — so "breadth deteriorated" becomes "Semiconductors are washed out at p2 while Financials sit at p97."
